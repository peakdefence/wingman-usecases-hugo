This site is published by Peak Defence. It sets no cookies, loads nothing from advertising or analytics companies, and keeps its visitor statistics itself.

## What is counted

- The page you view, the sections of a page that scroll into view, and whether a prompt was copied.
- The website you came from, as a domain name only, when your browser sends one.
- Your country, derived by the hosting provider from your network address.
- A visitor token for the current day: a one-way hash of your network address and browser signature mixed with a random value that is generated fresh every day and deleted the day after. It lets us tell one visitor from ten on the same day. It cannot be reversed, is never written to your device, and is replaced by a plain count within two days.

No names, no e-mail addresses, no cross-site identifiers, no advertising profiles. The counts are kept as daily totals and used only to see which content is useful.

## What is stored on your device

The data model explorer remembers which view you last chose, in your browser's local storage, so the page opens the same way next time. If you opt out below, that choice is stored the same way. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is read back for tracking.

Videos are embedded from youtube-nocookie.com and load only after you press play. From that moment YouTube's own terms apply.

## Legal basis and hosting

The counter runs on the site's hosting provider, Netlify, which also serves the pages and processes your network address for that purpose. We rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the published material is used, in a way designed to have no meaningful effect on you. Your browser's Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal is honoured automatically.
