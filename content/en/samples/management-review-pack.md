**Example Co.**, a 120-person software company. **Review:** Q3 2026, held on 14 October 2026. **Prepared from the records on:** 13 October 2026.

## Decisions requested

1. **Accept a residual risk.** "Only one engineer can operate the cloud platform" stays at score 9 (likelihood 3, impact 3) until a second engineer is trained, planned for Q1 2027. Proposed by the risk owner, the Head of Engineering.
2. **Approve a policy.** Access management policy, version 4. What changed: quarterly review of admin accounts, removal of shared accounts.
3. **Fund a test.** An external penetration test of the customer portal in Q4. The estimate is attached to the task.

## Where we stand

From the Brief, written by WINGMAN's AI from the records on 13 October: risk is concentrated on the customer portal and the identity provider it depends on. 41 of 47 applicable controls are operating with valid evidence. Three controls have evidence that expires before the next review.

## Recorded after the meeting

The minutes are a document record: what was presented, what was decided, and who approved it. Decision 1 accepted; decision 2 approved; decision 3 approved with a budget limit. Each decision became a task with an owner and a due date, and unfinished ones return at the next review. Every change carries the person who made it and the time, and the history cannot be edited or deleted.
