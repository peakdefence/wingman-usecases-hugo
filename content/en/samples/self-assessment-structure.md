**Structural example, not the regulator's template.** It shows what WINGMAN puts together from the records and where each part comes from. When the report is submitted, these fields go into the regulator's own template; requirement numbers are shown as [x] here and come from the loaded requirement records in WINGMAN.

**Example Agency**, a fictional public body with 250 staff, an essential entity under the National Cybersecurity Law. **Period:** 2026. **Prepared from the records on:** 20 November 2026 by the cybersecurity manager, R. Grava.

## Requirements and fulfilment

| Requirement | Proposed level | Why | Controls and evidence | Gaps, as tasks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regulation No. 397, p. [x]: cybersecurity manager appointed | Met | Appointed by order of the head; deputy named | CTL-01; order of 12 Feb 2026 | – |
| p. [x]: risk management | Partly met | Risks assessed for 4 of 6 services | CTL-04; risk register, review of 3 Nov | Assess 2 remaining services, due 15 Jan |
| p. [x]: backups | Met | Daily backups, restore tested quarterly | CTL-11; restore test of 22 Oct | – |
| p. [x]: encryption | Partly met | Laptops encrypted; backups not encrypted at rest | CTL-12; device report of 18 Nov | Encrypt backups, due 31 Jan |
| p. [x]: outsourcing and suppliers | Not met | 3 of 7 critical suppliers have no security requirements in the contract | CTL-15 | Amend 3 contracts, due 31 Mar |
| p. [x]: incident classification and reporting | Met | Classified against the criteria; reports sent on time | CTL-20; incident log, 2 incidents | – |

Proposed levels are a draft. The scale is replaced by the regulator's scale, and every level is confirmed by a person.

## What the report rests on

Each level links to its controls, their status and evidence valid on the date of the report. Requirements with no control, or with expired evidence, are listed as gaps before anyone signs.

## Approval

Reviewed by R. Grava (cybersecurity manager) on 25 November; approved by the head of the agency on 2 December. Both are recorded in the history with the time, which cannot be edited or deleted. The conclusion for each requirement is kept for next year's report.
