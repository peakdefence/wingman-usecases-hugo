## Your workspace

Each organisation has its own workspace, which WINGMAN calls a project. Requirements, assets, risks, controls, suppliers, evidence, tasks and documents live there as linked records. The data can be exported.

A consultant works in each client's workspace separately. Skills a consultant builds remain the consultant's property; they are not available to Peak Defence and are hidden from other consultants.

## Which AI works on your records

- **Inside WINGMAN.** The briefs, Ask Wingman and the suggestions are written by a model set up for your organisation. Depending on that configuration it runs in the cloud, in a cloud restricted to the EU, or entirely in a local deployment, and Peak Defence can provide the model.
- **Your own assistant.** When you connect Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant over MCP, the model is the one in your assistant, under your own agreement with its provider.
- **Web search.** When an assistant looks something up, it uses WINGMAN's built-in search or an external search service, kept apart from your records.

## Who can reach what

- Permissions are set for each person and each type of record, for reading, writing and deleting.
- An assistant connected over MCP signs in as a person, with OAuth or a token, and has exactly that person's permissions. Further controls can be added to a connection, and a connection can be restricted to a single workspace or narrower.
- A read-only role can be configured for an external auditor.

## What is kept

Every change to a record is kept in its history: what changed, the value before and after, when, and who made it. The time is set by the system, and the history cannot be edited or deleted. Accepting an AI suggestion is recorded as its own entry. Documents and other attachments keep their versions.
