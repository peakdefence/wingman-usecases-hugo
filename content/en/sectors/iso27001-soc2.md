summary: Keep the whole ISMS in one model, with control ownership, recurring reviews, management review and evidence that holds up over an audit period.
audience: Software, SaaS and service companies certifying to ISO 27001 or reporting under SOC 2 Type 2, often both at once.
standards:
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022
- SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria
- ISO 27001 Annex A
stages:
- title: Scope and context
  text: Products, services, systems and data in scope are assets, with their dependencies and the suppliers behind them.
  nodes:
  - asset
  - supplier
  - document
  links:
  - asset-asset
  - supplier-asset
  - document-asset
  ai: Drafts the scope statement and the system description from the asset records.
  decides: Management approves the scope.
- title: One control set, two frameworks
  text: Annex A controls and Trust Services Criteria are requirements mapped to each other. Each control is written once and meets both.
  nodes:
  - requirement
  - control
  - document
  links:
  - requirement-requirement
  - requirement-control
  - control-document
  ai: Maps criteria to existing controls and lists criteria with no control.
  decides: The ISMS owner confirms.
- title: Real-time Statement of Applicability
  text: The Statement of Applicability is generated on the fly from the controls actually in operation. For each Annex A control it shows applicability and its justification, the risks it treats, its implementation status and whether valid evidence proves it runs today.
  nodes:
  - requirement
  - control
  - risk
  - evidence
  - document
  links:
  - requirement-control
  - control-risk
  - control-evidence
  - requirement-document
  ai: Rebuilds the SoA whenever a control, risk or piece of evidence changes, and flags controls marked as implemented that have no valid evidence.
  decides: The ISMS owner approves each published version.
- title: Control ownership
  text: Every control has an owner role, a frequency and the evidence it produces. People change; roles keep the controls owned.
  nodes:
  - role
  - person
  - control
  links:
  - person-role
  - role-control
  ai: Lists controls with no owner and roles with no holder.
  decides: Management assigns.
- title: Recurring reviews
  text: Asset, risk, document, access and supplier reviews are planned on their cycle. Each run produces a task and evidence.
  nodes:
  - plan
  - asset
  - risk
  - document
  - task
  links:
  - plan-asset
  - plan-risk
  - plan-document
  - task-plan
  ai: Creates the review tasks on schedule and chases overdue ones.
  decides: Owners complete the reviews.
- title: Evidence over the period
  text: SOC 2 Type 2 tests that controls operated across the whole period. Evidence carries the period it covers and when it expires.
  nodes:
  - control
  - evidence
  - task
  links:
  - control-evidence
  - control-task
  ai: Shows gaps in the period before the auditor samples it.
  decides: Control owners provide evidence.
- title: Internal audit and management review
  text: The internal audit tests clauses and controls. Management review takes its results, risks, incidents and objectives, and its decisions become tasks.
  nodes:
  - audit
  - finding
  - conclusion
  - document
  - task
  links:
  - audit-finding
  - finding-conclusion
  - finding-task
  - document-task
  ai: Prepares the audit pack and the management review inputs from current records.
  decides: Top management decides.
registers:
- entity: asset
  title: Asset inventory
  text: Information and associated assets with owners and classification, reviewed on a schedule (A.5.9).
- entity: risk
  title: Risk register and treatment plan
  text: Risks with owners, ratings and treatments, linked to the controls in the Statement of Applicability.
- entity: requirement
  title: Live Statement of Applicability
  text: Generated from the control register, not kept as a separate spreadsheet. Applicability, justification, linked risks, status and evidence per Annex A control, with approved versions kept for the auditor.
- entity: control
  title: Control register
  text: Annex A and Trust Services Criteria controls with owners, frequency, status and evidence.
- entity: document
  title: Document register
  text: Policies and procedures with version, owner, approval and review date.
- entity: person
  title: People and access
  text: Staff and contractors with roles, training, policy acknowledgements and the access they hold, for joiner, mover and leaver checks and access reviews.
- entity: supplier
  title: Supplier register
  text: Suppliers and subservice organisations with the services they provide, assessments and contracts.
- entity: evidence
  title: Evidence library
  text: Proof per control and period, approved by the owner, with expiry, ready for sampling.
- entity: plan
  title: Compliance calendar
  text: Reviews, internal audits, management reviews, tests and trainings as one yearly plan.
use_cases:
- uc: regular-reviews
  text: Asset, risk, document and access reviews run on schedule, each leaving a task and evidence behind.
- uc: roles-raci
  text: Control ownership is explicit and segregation of duties conflicts show up as gaps.
- uc: auditing
  text: Internal audits per clause with findings, conclusions and corrective actions, ready for the certification body or the SOC 2 auditor.
- uc: audit-scopes
  text: One audit programme covering several products, or ISO 27001 and SOC 2 at once, without duplicate evidence.
- uc: management-review
  text: Inputs required by clause 9.3 come from live records; decisions become tracked tasks.
- uc: risk-review
  text: Risk assessment and treatment on a set cycle. A treatment decision changes the live Statement of Applicability straight away.
- uc: change-management
  text: Changes are assessed for security impact, and the change record is evidence for change management controls.
- uc: security-operations
  text: Operational controls such as backups, logging and patching produce evidence every time they run.
questions:
- q: Did every control operate for the whole audit period?
  a: From each control to its evidence by period, showing gaps and expired items before sampling begins.
- q: What does our Statement of Applicability say today?
  a: It is generated from the controls in operation at this moment, so status and justification match reality. Controls claimed as implemented without valid evidence are flagged before the auditor sees them.
- q: Which reviews are overdue?
  a: From the compliance calendar to each review task, its owner and due date.
- q: Who owns this control now that its owner has left?
  a: Ownership sits on the role. Assign the role to a successor and the control, its tasks and its evidence follow.
- q: Are we ready for the management review?
  a: Audit results, risk changes, incidents, objectives and open actions are pulled from records, not collected by email.
---

ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type 2 ask for the same thing in different words: a defined scope, controls with owners, and proof that they operate. SOC 2 Type 2 adds a period: evidence must cover the whole observation window, not only the day of the audit.

WINGMAN holds the whole ISMS as linked records: assets, risks, controls, documents, people, suppliers, evidence and the calendar of reviews. Because Annex A and the Trust Services Criteria map to the same controls, one set of evidence serves both. The Statement of Applicability is not a document someone updates once a year: it is generated in real time from the controls actually in operation, their linked risks and their evidence.
